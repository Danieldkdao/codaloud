# Codaloud vendor cost source notes

Retrieved **2026-09-29**. USD unless stated otherwise. These are public list prices, not Codaloud's actual invoices or proposed retail prices. Provider prices and plan entitlements can change; confirm them again before publishing prices. All vendor claims below link to first-party pages. The code references identify the current billable paths, including code that is present but not necessarily exercised by users.

The Expo API server / deployment host, notification delivery, and any purchased domain or tunneling service also have potential cost, but **this repo does not identify a production hosting plan or traffic volume**. No defensible per-user amount is available yet. GitHub OAuth/repository API and local on-device editor/project operations are not assigned per-call vendor costs in these sources.

**Fixed plan fees versus marginal overages:** LiveKit Ship's $50/month and Scale's $500/month are fixed plan starts that buy included monthly allotments; $0.01/agent minute and $0.0005/WebRTC minute are **only after their respective Ship allowances**. Trigger Hobby/Pro base fees likewise include equal monthly usage credits. Firecrawl paid plans buy monthly credits, with separately priced opt-in extra credits. In customer margin scenarios, allocate the fixed bill once across subscribers and use marginal rates only after the relevant shared allowance is exhausted; do not add a plan fee once per customer action. [LiveKit](https://livekit.com/pricing), [Trigger.dev](https://trigger.dev/pricing), [Firecrawl](https://www.firecrawl.dev/pricing)

## Store and billing deductions from retail price

| Platform | Current published fee rule relevant to Codaloud |
| --- | --- |
| **Apple App Store** | Standard commission **30%**; qualifying auto-renewing subscriptions after a year **15%**; developers approved for the **App Store Small Business Program** get **15%** on eligible sales. Apple Developer Program is **$99/year**. [Program fee summary](https://developer.apple.com/programs/whats-included/), [license terms and eligibility](https://developer.apple.com/support/terms/apple-developer-program-license-agreement/) |
| **Google Play** | As of **2026-06-30** for US, UK, and EEA users, the fee is region/install dependent. Auto-renewing subscriptions for first $1M annual earnings: **10% service fee plus 5% Google Play Billing fee**. Other transactions in that tier: new installs **10% + 5%**; existing installs under standard fee **20% + 5%**. Outside those markets pending rollout: enrolled 15% tier **15% on first $1M**, 30% beyond; auto-renewing subscriptions **15%**. [Google Play service fees](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622?hl=en-GB) |
| **RevenueCat** | Free below **$2,500 monthly tracked revenue**. Once threshold is met, Pro charges **1% of all that month's MTR**, not only dollars above $2,500; its basis is before store commission and tax. [Pricing](https://www.revenuecat.com/pricing), [billing examples](https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/welcome/set-up-revenuecat/account-management) |

For gross margin, calculate per purchase as **retail receipts less actual store fee, RevenueCat fee when applicable, variable vendor usage, and allocated fixed infrastructure**. Do not use a universal 15% store deduction across every geography and product type. Trial credits and other giveaways still incur vendor expense. Taxes, refunds, chargebacks, negotiated vendor rates, and currency conversion require separate accounting.

## Evidence still needed before approving customer prices

1. Representative distributions: input/output tokens for quick edits, voice replies, and full agent tasks; Deepgram audio minutes; ElevenLabs generated characters; LiveKit connected minutes; Firecrawl searches/scrapes; Daytona sandbox started/stopped GB-hours; Trigger billable compute seconds. Prefer p50, p90, p99 and averages by paid tier rather than one synthetic example.
2. Actual vendor account plan and provider routing: OpenRouter provider invoice rate, ElevenLabs account billing mode, Deepgram promo expiry, Daytona default sandbox resources, LiveKit deployment tier, Neon paid/free status, Trigger worker size.
3. Target store mix: iOS/Android, market, subscription versus consumable, Small Business eligibility, and expected refund/tax treatment. These materially change net receipts.
4. Hosting deployment and app-store account fees, plus any support or human operations cost, before claiming net profitability.
